The Rec

Privacy policy

Short version: The Rec doesn't collect anything about you. Your club lives on your phone.

Who we are

The Rec is a football game for iPhone, made by BlackmoreApps. If you have a question about this policy, email rob@the-rec-game.com.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The Rec doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your club, your season, the names you type and the photos you choose for your mates or your badge are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app.

Photos you pick are copied into the app so they keep working. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Your first season is free. If you buy the Full Club, the payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only which purchases you've made, so it can unlock them, and restore them on a new phone. Apple's own privacy policy covers the payment.

Notifications

If you turn on Sunday reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time from the Club tab or your phone's Settings.

Sharing and backups

When you share a card or save a backup, it goes only where you choose through your phone's share sheet (for example Files, iCloud Drive or a messaging app). That service's own privacy policy then applies.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and contain nothing that identifies you.

Children

Because The Rec collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

This website

These pages use no cookies, no analytics and nothing loaded from other websites. Like any website, the hosting service may keep basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the app will tell you. Questions? Email rob@the-rec-game.com.
