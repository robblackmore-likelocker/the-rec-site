Is The Rec free?

Your whole first season is free, with no adverts: building the club, a full league season, the cup and the end-of-season night at the Ferret.

To carry on into Season 2 and beyond (the whole ten-season story and everything after it), buy the Full Club once, for £5.99 or the same in your own currency. It's a one-off purchase, not a subscription, and you can never buy club money or better players.

I've bought the Full Club. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open The Rec, open the menu and tap Restore purchases. If you use Family Sharing, the Full Club is shared with your family too.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

How do I move my club to a new iPhone?

If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your club should come across with it.

The surest way: in The Rec, open the menu, tap Backups, then Back up my club. That saves one file with your whole club and every photo inside it. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, open Backups and tap Restore from a file.

I deleted the app. Can I get my club back?

Only from a backup file you saved, or a backup of your iPhone made before you deleted it. We never had a copy, because your club never leaves your phone.

How do I turn Sunday reminders on or off?

On the Club tab, find Sunday morning reminders and tap On or Off. You can also turn notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.

Why does The Rec ask for my photos?

Only when you choose to add a picture for one of your mates or your badge. The photo is copied into the app and never uploaded. You can change photo access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Is my data safe?

The Rec doesn't collect anything about you: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.
